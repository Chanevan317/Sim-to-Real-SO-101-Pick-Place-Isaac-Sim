How to record

        
        
    
          
  1. Start and end every try with the leader arm in its home pose.
    2. 
          
  2. Read the TARGET label in the simulator: move only that cube, leave the other one alone.
    3. 
          
  3. Close the gripper firmly on the cube, all the way.
    4. 
          
  4. Put the cube inside the plate, open the gripper, then bring the arm back to its home pose.
    5. 
          
  5. Once the arm is back home, press N to save. Nothing is saved until you press N.
    6. 
          
  6. Press X to throw a try away if you drop the cube, touch the wrong cube, hit the plate or a wall, or pause for long.
    7. 
          
  7. Move smoothly, at a steady pace.
    8. 
          
  8. Click the simulator window first, so it receives N and X.
    9. 
          
  9. If Ubuntu says the simulator is not responding while it opens, click Wait or leave it. Never Force Quit.
